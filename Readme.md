#  MyDuitAI

> **Predict financial stress before it becomes a crisis.**

---

##  Problem

Young Malaysians are increasingly falling into debt:

- **53,000** individuals under 30 carry nearly **RM1.9 billion** in debt  
- **26%** of bankruptcy cases involve people below 34  
- BNPL usage is rapidly rising:
  - **102.6 million transactions**
  - **RM9.3 billion in value**
- **40%** of BNPL users are aged 30 and below  

###  Why current solutions fail:

- Reactive — show damage **after overspending**
- Depend on **user self-control**
- Intervene **too late (at checkout or after)**

---

##  Solution: MyDuitAI

**MyDuitAI** is an **AI-powered financial wellbeing platform** that predicts financial distress **up to 60 days in advance** — and intervenes early enough to prevent it.

> Instead of asking *“What did you spend?”*  
> We ask: **“Where are you heading?”**

---

##  How It Works

###  3-Layer Intelligent System

---

###  1. Predict (60 Days Before Crisis)

The AI continuously monitors:

-  BNPL usage frequency  
-  End-of-month balance trend  
-  Repayment punctuality  
-  BNPL debt-to-income ratio  
-  Spending vs income ratio  

All signals combine into:

## **Financial Stress Score (0–100)**

> A predictive indicator of your future financial health — not just your current state.

---

###  2. Educate (30 Days Before Crisis)

When **Score = 40–59 (Warning Zone)**:

- Personalized **3-month cash flow forecast**
- AI explains **exact risk drivers**
- Shows **trend, not snapshot**

**Example:**

> *"Your BNPL usage is up 40%. At this pace, you'll have RM156 left in December."*

---

###  3. Intervene (At Checkout)

When risk is high:

- Real-time warning **before purchase**
- Default option: **⏸ Pause (not Proceed)**

#### User choices:
-  Pause 24 hours  
-  View full forecast  
-  Proceed anyway  

---

###  Critical Threshold (Score < 30)

- BNPL spending is **soft-locked**
- Automatic connection to **AKPK debt counselling**

---

##  Key Features

-  **Financial Stress Score**  
  Predicts financial distress before it happens  

-  **Predictive Cash Flow Forecast**  
  Shows where your current behavior leads  

-  **Behavioral Learning AI**  
  Adapts to each user’s habits over time  

-  **Friction-by-Default Design**  
  Makes impulsive spending harder  

-  **Social Proof Nudges**  
  Compare your behavior with similar peers  

-  **Auto AKPK Escalation**  
  Connects users to real help before crisis
